Submission Steps
1
Open the App Review panel
  1. Go to developers.facebook.com → My Apps → select your Softmarche app
  2. In the left sidebar, click App Review → Permissions and Features
  3. In the search box, type instagram_manage_messages
  4. Click Request Advanced Access next to it
2
Fill in the Use Case description RequiredCopy below

Meta will ask: "How will you use this permission?" — paste this exact text:

Softmarche is an internal social media management tool used by our hospitality group to manage Instagram accounts for 80+ hotel properties across India. We are requesting instagram_manage_messages to access inbox analytics for our managed hotel accounts. Specifically, we need to: 1. Count the total number of DMs received per property per month 2. Measure our response rate within 24 hours (a key hospitality KPI) 3. Categorize incoming messages by topic (Room bookings, Food & Beverage enquiries, MICE/Events, Collaboration requests, and General) so our social media team can report to individual hotel clients on the type of enquiries their Instagram presence is generating We do NOT intend to send automated messages or respond to DMs via the API. We are only reading message metadata and content for analytics and reporting purposes. All data stays within our internal dashboard and is never shared with third parties. This data is displayed in a monthly PDF report that is shared with each hotel client, helping them understand the business impact of their Instagram activity beyond just likes and reach.

Record a screen recording (video demo) Required

Meta requires a screen recording showing exactly how you use the permission. Record a 2–3 minute video showing:

  1. Open Softmarche dashboard at softmarche.cinuniverse.com
  2. Navigate to Analytics page
  3. Select a hotel property from the dropdown
  4. Narrate (or add text overlay): "This is our analytics dashboard. We want to add a DM section here showing inbox volume, response rate, and message categories for this property."

💡 Use Loom (free) or your phone screen recorder. Upload to Google Drive or YouTube (unlisted) and paste the link in the submission form.
4
Complete the Data Use Checkboxes

Meta will show a list of checkboxes about how you'll use the data. Select these:

  • ✅ Provide analytics or insights to businesses
  • ✅ Provide a social media management tool
  • ❌ Do NOT check "Send messages on behalf of the business" (you're only reading)
  • ❌ Do NOT check "Automate responses"
5
Privacy Policy — what it must mention

Your privacy policy (at your Privacy Policy URL) must explicitly mention message data. Add this paragraph to your privacy policy page:

Message Data: Softmarche accesses Instagram Direct Message metadata (message count, timestamps, and content) for analytics and reporting purposes only. This data is used to generate monthly performance reports for hotel clients. Message content is processed by AI to categorize enquiry types (e.g., room bookings, dining enquiries). No message data is stored permanently, shared with third parties, or used for advertising purposes. Message data is retained for a maximum of 90 days for reporting purposes.
6
Submit and wait
  1. Click Submit for Review
  2. Meta will acknowledge receipt — you'll get an email
  3. Review typically takes 5–7 business days
  4. You may get a follow-up question from Meta — respond within 24 hours to avoid delays

💡 If rejected: Meta will give a reason. The most common rejection is "video demo is not clear enough." Re-record with clearer narration and resubmit — resubmissions go faster (2–3 days).
